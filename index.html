👀 For Grown-Ups

STEM Star Club is a free collection of bite-sized Science, Technology, Engineering and Math games designed for ages 5–9.

  • ✅ 100% safe: no ads, no chat, no sign-up, no links out to the internet.
  • ✅ Private: everything (names, stickers, progress) is saved only on this device — nothing is ever uploaded.
  • ✅ Works offline once the page has loaded.
  • ✅ Encouraging by design: mistakes are met with kindness, not penalties.

Each child gets their own profile. The age you choose sets the difficulty automatically (younger = gentler). The "🔥 streak" rewards coming back to learn a little every day.
